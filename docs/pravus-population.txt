point anywhere on an intersecting polygon, outside the requested radius. A

it does not rewrite their pool centers/radii or introduce new patrol routes.
PravusFrontlinesPopulationTests samples all four new footprints, checks reachable
ground and the room's lower floor/stair clearance, and exercises SQLite upgrade,
repeat upgrade, preservation/rollback and offline MySQL SQL generation.

Respawn behavior (important):
spawnpool.respown_time is in tenths of a second. New guards use 1200 (120 s),
Linkers 1800 (180 s). Those are fallback timers when squad instancing is off.
Normal squad instances override per-pool timers: the current default is
45 minutes after the entire pool is cleared, via SquadInstances.RespawnMinutes.
This patch preserves that policy and does not make normal Pravus instances
respawn every two/three minutes. Respawn is per cleared pool, not per death.

V4 playtest correction - October 20, 2026
----------------------------------------
The user pulled all three new Frontlines Machina groups on the same road.
V3 centers were only 46.1 and 56.6 m apart. Default detection is 18 m and
creatures may chase 60 m from home; idle wandering stays within the pool.
Migration 20261220000000_Pravus_frontlines_spacing_v4 moves only those pools:
  640202 central approach: (-80, 25.213156, 10), radius 10 m
  640203 southern field:  (-90, 36.395542, -100), radius 12 m
  640204 northern field:  (-60, 25.937, 115), radius 12 m
Adjacent centers are now 106.9 and 110.5 m apart. Counts remain 4-6 each,
level 10, and respawn settings/instance policy are unchanged. This is an
estimated encounter layout, not recovered retail coordinates. Four hundred
navmesh samples per pool were reachable and inside their radii; pool footprints
clear the existing AFS turret scan range. In-game terrain and encounter spacing
still need a walk-through. Pulling enemies deliberately between packs can
still combine encounters. V4 rollback restores the exact V3 coordinates/radii.

The same playtest reported corpses firing until removed, including Thrax as
well as Machina. Client 1.16.5.0 actor.pyo shows Recv_StateChange only calls
TransitionTo; AnnounceDeath also cancels the current action, stops tracking,
hides weapons, and stops persistent effects. Recv_ActorKilled invokes that
cleanup only if IsDead is false. Creature deaths previously sent StateChange
without ActorKilled. Both ordinary death and Machina's deferred first death
now send ActorKilled BEFORE StateChange, and stop movement. Pending attacks
from that creature are marked cancelled without mutating the missile queue
while a killing hit may be iterating it. Explicit in-flight projectiles remain
in flight. Cancellation survives revival, so a queued old attack cannot start
again when its caster rises. This corrects a demonstrated notification gap;
confirmation that it resolves the entire reported symptom requires a client
retest. Existing dying-state AI guards and Machina self-revival are retained.
Local disassembly, navigation samples and database backup are kept outside
Git in Pravus Research/population-v4; no client assets are included in the patch.
