PRAVUS RESEARCH: MISSION RECONSTRUCTION SPECIFICATION
21 October 2026 | First playable implementation and historical evidence

PURPOSE

Restore Pravus as a squad operation with linked missions, encounter progression,
reinforcements, access control and a production-machine finale. The earlier
population-only encounter-plan-v1 is a survey/design reference, not the final
spawn specification. Do not combine its entire static population with new
scripted waves. Migration 20261221000000_Pravus_operation_v1 supersedes the
temporary population with the operation described below. The evidence ledger
later in this document preserves the distinction between recovered facts and
the reconstruction decisions made for this first pass.

FIRST PLAYABLE PASS

Content revision: pravus_operation_v1, World databases only (SQLite/MySQL).
The main route is 570 Traitors to the Cause -> 574 Machinations -> 593 The
Escapist -> 575 The Means of Production -> 1075 A Higher Calling. Existing
570/574 data are preserved. The player still has to loot ten actual Forean
remains outdoors, deliver them to Parsons, accept 593, and speak with Nylla.
The usual completed-starting-experience qualification applies.

Price offers 321 independently; Perkins completes it after 15 Frontlines
Forean Machina kills. The shared instance delivers five per Bane dropship,
waits 15 seconds after the last dies, and continues while an eligible living
player is within 60 m of the Frontlines entrance. Uncredited kills do not exhaust
the event. Three AFS soldiers hold the sandbags. Northern/southern field groups
have 4 Machina each; a far-north group has 3. These counts/timings are estimates.

Two shootable security cameras watch the bridge. A six-second alarm delay
allows both to be destroyed; otherwise one level 11 Juggernaut arrives. The
entrance has three key-bearing Thrax plus one grenadier. The key is personal
mission loot: template 11505, class 20000013, 100% chance while 575 objective 3
needs it. Eligible nearby squad members each receive their own claimable copy,
including under Individual loot, and cannot take another player's key.
Killing without looting does not satisfy that objective. Existing
inventory satisfies it when an assignment is accepted again. The key survives
abandonment/reconnect and is consumed at 575 turn-in. Doors at the staircase
bulkhead and prison open once a squad member has actually collected a key.

The downstairs room has 3 soldiers and 1 grenadier, the passage 3 Linkers, and
the factory 3 groups of 4 Thrax (12 total). Factory guards activate when an
eligible player approaches. Production adds 3 Forean Machina every 25 seconds,
capped at 6 living Machina. Destroying the native fueling capsule stops further
production; the player must also kill the remaining factory guards/Machina.
Finale Nylla then appears to accept the mission. The village Nylla remains
available, so players cannot lose their hand-in contact. Maulis is an optional
prison conversation, not a mandatory boss or Linker kill gate.

Nylla issues the real armband (template 45439/class 22953) on accepting 1075.
It persists with the assignment and is consumed by the Baruhi conversation;
abandoning 1075 removes it. It cannot be replaced by a bare conversation flag.

Johnson offers 323 independently for this first pass because the late-retail
unlock condition is uncertain. All three radar structures survive in the
shipped map, class 3548:
  North objective 309:     (-2.861,47.394,298.668)
  Northwest objective 310:(-54.641,32.858,163.034)
  South objective 311:    (-47.340,36.385,-224.068)
These native landmarks resolve the guide's repeated/mistyped third coordinate.
One shootable infestation per dish is provisional. Destroy all three and
return to Johnson. Objective completion is shown; dynamic remaining-enemy
counter text is not yet restored for 323/575.

Standley's 924 dispatch uses the existing real Communication 51, Control 41 and
Machine 34 shrines. Already learned Logos count. It does not gate the factory.

TESTING AND KNOWN FIRST-PASS LIMITS

PravusOperationTests exercises native NPC offers/conversations/turn-ins,
actual damage/corpse pickup, key and armband persistence, all-clear gating,
dropship caps, radio objectives, Logos use and camera/instance isolation.
PravusOperationMigrationTests checks provider SQL generation and exact rollback
of the affected data tables, including foreign-key integrity.

575 and 323 currently require each player to accept from the NPC; the journal's
share action is disabled for these two scripted assignments. Their world
encounters remain shared and eligible participants earn their own progress.
The declared data.sequence signals use the existing durable scene delivery
path; the map controller owns spawning, so each assignment creates no extra mobs.

Player objectives/inventory/rewards are durable. The encounter objects and
wave timers reset if their map is unloaded or the server restarts. Resuming
players in the same instance identity can complete the rebuilt encounter;
this is not retail instance-save persistence. Scene-backed assignments remain
bound to the instance where they were accepted. If changing squads or resetting
the instance gives a different instance identity, abandon/reaccept 575 or 323
from its NPC. The retained key satisfies the new 575 assignment. Destroyed
facts are reconciled for eligible players revisiting
the same running instance. A full wipe does not create a second factory or
unbounded reinforcements. Cleared ordinary pools retain normal squad respawn
policy (which can override the 180-second database delay).

Factory/capsule assembly origins, camera height, door fit, infestation visual
fit, Maulis presentation, combat tuning and enemy sightlines require client
playtesting. Native components are used, but exact server placements were
not recovered. AFS movement/cinematic timing and the village Nylla's staged
departure are not recreated. The Juggernaut uses native guns/eyebeam actions
with estimated damage; additional retail abilities remain future refinement.

ENCOUNTER SEQUENCE AND PROPOSED SERVER RULES

A. Camp / briefing
   Restore593 turn-in and575 offer at Nylla;321 at Price. Retain services.
   Real NPC conversation packages must be bound to the restored creatures.
   Keep quest offerings distinct from simply entering the map.

B. Frontlines battle
   H: sandbag defense, allied troops and incoming dropships;15 qualifying kills.
   R initial design: eligible squad member with active321 enters the grounded
     Frontlines trigger volume; one shared encounter starts for that instance.
     Use three drops of five enemies with at most five encounter enemies alive
     at once. These counts/conditions are estimates, NOT recovered retail rules.
   R: schedule another drop after the preceding wave dies, with a short
     readable arrival interval; exact timing needs aircraft-animation validation.
   Credit nearby eligible squad members under existing mission-credit rules.
   Recovery must not strand progress when allies kill enemies, a player dies,
     someone arrives late, or the server reloads. A restartable attempt or
     additional bounded wave must supply missing eligible kills without double
     credit. Choose and test that policy before enabling the scene.
   Remove/suppress the overlapping static entry pack when enabling this battle.
   F2/F3/far-north field groups are separate ambient proposals, not extra waves.

C. Outer battlefield / communications
   Keep separated approach encounters and AFS defenses; add real destroyable
   infestation to mapped dishes for323 once its unlock/mapping is resolved.
   Do not stack enemy spawn radii into the friendly fortifications.

D. Bridge / entrance
   H: two cameras; failure to deal with them risks a Juggernaut; a small Thrax
     group carries the access key.
   R: bounded detection/alarm sequence creates at most one Juggernaut per
     encounter attempt; both cameras destroyed before alarm prevents it.
   U: original detection range, delay, patrol/landing position and repeat rule.
   Resolve the native keypass template and actual access terminal/door. Key
     acquisition must grant genuine mission item credit; door state is shared
     within the squad instance, not separately opened per character.

E. Interior / prisoner / Linkers
   Retain the user-confirmed guard room below the stairs. Population should
     follow room roles; earlier proposed guard counts remain estimates.
   Restore optional Maulis conversation in a verified prison-cell position.
   H: three Linkers in the passage near X/Z139.7,98.4.
   Do not require Linker deaths for575: the guide explicitly could not verify
     that condition and the client objectives do not specify it.
   Reflection behavior needs its own native-compatible combat implementation.

F. Production chamber
   Discovering the source advances575.1 and exposes the capsule objective.
   H/N: shoot the exposed capsule; it stops Machina production.
   H: approximately twelve Thrax defend the final room.
   R: start with twelve total guards and a cap of three active produced Machina;

     objectives/clear condition, then offer the1075 epilogue.
   H:575 pays1050 credits and an EMP pistol. Resolve a working appropriate

ENGINEERING BOUNDARIES

- Use existing normalized mission rows and typed scene interfaces, paired
  SQLite/MySQL data migrations and fixed migration helpers. Do not mutate
  already-applied V1-V4 population migrations or add another quest engine.
- Keep squad-instance encounter state separate from each player's assignment.
  Multiple accepts must not multiply dropships, doors, capsule or Nylla.
- Use native IDs and packages; resolve item-template IDs independently from
  class IDs. Mission inventory must use assignment/party semantics supported
  by the existing mission-item system, not ad-hoc character inventory writes.
- Preserve outdoor570/574 and their existing completed progress. Add593 after
  the boundary; do not replace outdoor quests with instance shortcuts.
- Separate authored server choices from recovered evidence in migration notes.
- Factory and access-object positions have been authored provisionally for
  testing; their visual fit and collision remain native-client checks.
- Do not deploy against a running test session. A restart can auto-apply pending
  SQLite migrations, so code readiness and local deployment are distinct steps.
